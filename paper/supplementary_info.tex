\section{Statistical Analysis}

\subsection{Precursor Rate Statistics}

The precursor detection rate of 73.3\% (22/30 trials) was evaluated for statistical significance using a binomial test against the null hypothesis of random ordering (50\% rate):

\begin{equation}
p = P(X \geq 22 | n=30, p_0=0.5) = \sum_{k=22}^{30} \binom{30}{k} (0.5)^{30} \approx 0.016
\end{equation}

This yields $p < 0.02$, rejecting the null hypothesis at the 5\% significance level.

\subsection{Gap Distribution}

For the 22 trials with detected precursors:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Mean gap: 750.8 steps
    \item Standard deviation: 1041.3 steps
    \item Median gap: 750 steps
    \item Interquartile range: [250, 1000] steps
\end{itemize}

The high variance reflects the stochastic nature of the nucleation process and the inherent fluctuations in finite-size systems.
